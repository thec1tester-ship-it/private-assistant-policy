Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2027

Application: Private Assistant (Alam Dunyo Edition)

Developer: THE C1 Technology

Core Privacy Principle: Private Assistant is built on the philosophy of Personal Data Sovereignty. Your data belongs solely to you. The application operates 100% offline-first, encrypts data on your device, and collects zero personal data.

1. Data Storage & Local Encryption

All credentials, passwords, financial records, notes, agendas, creative writing entries, and chat histories generated within Private Assistant are stored exclusively on your device's internal storage.

  • Sensitive data is encrypted using military-grade AES-256 GCM encryption.
  • Encryption keys are protected using Android KeyStore and hardware-backed biometric security (when enabled).
  • No data is ever uploaded, transmitted, or synchronized to any cloud server managed by THE C1 Technology.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute data privacy. THE C1 Technology does NOT collect, track, store, or share:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (Name, Email, Phone Number, Address).
  • Device Telemetry, Analytics, or Usage Statistics.
  • Passwords, Credentials, Financial Data, or Personal Notes.
  • Location Data or Contacts.

3. External AI Services & Web Sandbox

Private Assistant includes optional integrations for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Web Hub features:

  • User API Keys: If you input your own API keys (e.g., Google Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Anthropic Claude, Meta Llama/Groq), API requests are transmitted directly from your device to the respective provider's endpoint. Your API keys are encrypted locally and never sent to us.
  • AI Web Hub: Web portals opened within the AI Web Hub operate inside an isolated web container. We do not inspect, log, or track your browsing activity.

4. Data Backup & Export

You have total control over your data. You can export encrypted backups at any time. Backup files remain encrypted with AES-256 and can only be restored using your designated passphrase or PIN.

5. Device Permissions

Private Assistant requests minimum device permissions solely for functional purposes:

  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC): To unlock your local vault using fingerprint or face recognition.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Solely for voice dictation (Speech-to-Text) upon user request. Audio is processed on-device and never stored or recorded.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): To deliver local agenda and reminder alerts to your device's status bar.
  • Internet (INTERNET): Required solely for fetching user-initiated AI API responses, web hub access, and Play Store in-app update checks.

6. Children's Privacy

Private Assistant does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for general audiences.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
